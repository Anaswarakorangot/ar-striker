How to place in your space (AR)

  1. Choose your preferred motion (Run, Walk, or Wave).
  2. Tap View Character in AR in Safari on your iPhone.
  3. Move your iPhone slowly towards a floor or table surface until detected.
  4. Tap to place, then walk around the character at real-world scale!

Supports Apple ARKit Quick Look on iOS Safari and interactive WebXR on compatible mobile browsers.
