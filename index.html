How to View in Augmented Reality (AR)

  1. Select your preferred motion action above (Run, Walk, or Wave).
  2. Tap View Character in AR on your mobile device (Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android).
  3. Point your device camera at the floor or table to detect a surface, then tap to place the character at real-world scale.
  4. In WebXR AR, the floating action buttons remain directly on your camera screen so you can switch motions anytime while walking around the character!
